The same service sells on four different arguments, and using the wrong one wastes the
meeting.

- **Startup** — cheaper capital and faster warehouse/securitisation diligence. Buyer: founder
  or CTO. Decision: over coffee.
- **SME / regulated small institution** — the function you cannot staff, bought rather than
  built. Buyer: president or compliance head. Driver: supervisory expectation and the
  internal-audit capability gap.
- **Mid (regional banks, mid insurers)** — independent second opinion on AI models newly
  moved into core operations, where the internal team has not kept pace.
- **Large / mega** — independence and specialist depth at the margin: model classes the
  internal MRM team lacks, during exam cycles.

One page each, in Japanese and English. The flagship paper is the common credential behind
all four; the tier determines which sentence opens.
