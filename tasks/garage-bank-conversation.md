The fastest available test of `note-sme-wedge`, with someone who will take the call.

Four questions, asked as a former colleague rather than a vendor:

1. What does the valuation/recovery model actually decide, and who built it?
2. Has anyone outside the company ever reviewed it?
3. **Has a funding counterparty, bank partner or investor ever asked whether it has been
   independently validated?** — this is the question the whole SME thesis rests on.
4. What would have made such a review useful rather than an obstacle?

If the answer to (3) is no across this and two or three similar firms, the demand at the
startup tier is latent rather than real, and the plan should weight toward
`org-shogaku-tanki-hoken` and `org-jp-ai-credit-vendors`, where the pressure is structural.

A completed engagement here would be the **first reference case** — worth doing at low or no
fee for that reason alone, provided it is scoped tightly and written up.
