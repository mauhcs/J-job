`note-competitive-landscape` claimed no specialist quantitative risk boutiques operate in
Japan. Fintech協会's ALM・リスク管理 category shows five, so that claim needs settling.

Read the service pages of KYCコンサルティング, コンプライアンス・データラボ,
ステラリスクコンサルティング, rubi labs, 株式会社レンズ and EduCare, and classify each as:
independent model validation, ALM advisory, compliance tooling, or systems implementation.
Record headcount and client type where stated.

**Two decisions depend on the answer.** If any of them sells independent validation, the
competitive thesis needs rewriting and the differentiation argument has to work against a
domestic incumbent rather than only against the Big 4. If none does, several become candidate
**partners** — a domestic entity of record with us as technical principal, which is also a
possible answer to `task-jp-foreign-validator-legality`.
