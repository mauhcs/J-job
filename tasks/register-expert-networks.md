Register with **GLG, AlphaSights, Guidepoint and Tegus** alongside `org-visasq`. An afternoon
of profile writing, no pipeline, no approach to design — the counterparty initiates.

Profile around what is actually scarce: model validation and model risk, HFT and market
microstructure, and on-chain rate and oracle models.

**Do not project income from this.** No data exists on how many calls an individual advisor
receives per month; it depends on profile, seniority and availability. Register, run it for
three months, and record what arrives — number of enquiries, from whom, on what topics, and
what was asked. That record answers two things desk research could not: whether institutions
in this region are worried about model validation at all, and who is asking.

Settle conflict-of-interest and MNPI rules before the first call, and check whether IPOR Labs
or TUJ constrain outside paid expert work (overlaps `task-tuj-affiliation-rules`).
