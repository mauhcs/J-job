Market rate for a Japanese 顧問 is roughly **¥200,000–350,000 per month**. Pre-revenue,
that is 12 months of committed cost against an unproven thesis — so the offer has to be
structured, not just budgeted.

Options to cost out:

- **成果報酬 / success fee** — supported natively by 顧問バンク. Ties the advisor's return to
  introductions that convert. Cheapest to carry, hardest to get a senior person to accept
  without a track record behind it.
- **Equity or revenue share in the boutique** — requires the entity to exist, and makes the
  advisor a principal rather than a 顧問. May be the honest framing given how much the plan
  depends on them.
- **Reduced retainer plus co-authorship** — the publishing stream is a genuine non-cash
  asset. Worth less to an ex-CRO than to an academic, but not nothing.
- **社外取締役 seat** — stronger signal to a bank's procurement than 顧問; sourced through
  パソナ JOB HUB. Only available once there is an entity with a board.

Depends on `task-jp-foreign-validator-legality`: if independence must be domiciled in
Japan, the advisor is a co-principal and this becomes a founding-terms conversation rather
than a compensation one.
