Expert-witness firms maintain practices in algorithmic and electronic trading and in
quantitative finance across rates, FX, credit and equity derivatives, staffed partly by people
holding faculty posts at Asian universities. They are searching for exactly this CV, and they
do the selling.

1. Submit a profile to SEDA Experts, Expert Institute and Cahn Litigation Services.
2. Ask directly: do they take Japan-based experts, for which jurisdictions, and what disputes
   in this region involve algorithmic trading or derivatives valuation?
3. Check IPOR and TUJ constraints on testifying work, and any conflict rules.

Episodic, high-rate, and the counterparty initiates — which is why this ranks above anything
requiring a pipeline. It is also a credential in itself: "has served as an expert witness"
reads differently to a Japanese risk committee than "runs a consultancy".
