MEXT's ¥97.7bn of joint research flows into 大学等 in Japan. **TUJ is a foreign university's
Japan campus, and whether it participates in that system at all is unknown** — which makes
this the single blocker on `note-research-as-product`.

Establish:

- Can TUJ be the contracting party to a 共同研究契約 or 受託研究契約 with a Japanese company?
- Is there an industry-liaison function, a standard contract template, and an indirect-cost
  (間接経費) policy? Japanese universities typically take ~30% of direct costs.
- What may an **adjunct** faculty member do — lead a funded project, be named PI, or only
  participate?
- How is IP handled, and how is the revenue split between university and researcher?

Fold into `task-tuj-affiliation-rules`; one conversation likely answers both.

**If the answer is no**, the fallback is contracting personally, with the affiliation appearing
only as a byline on the resulting paper. That is materially weaker — the client is buying
institutional credibility and a personal consultancy does not supply it — but it is not
nothing, and it costs the client less in overhead.

**A second fallback worth pricing:** partner with a Japanese university that already has the
apparatus, via a co-author there. That is the same relationship as the academic 顧問 in
`person-archetype-academic-bridge` — which stops being a demoted archetype and becomes the
commercial enabler.
