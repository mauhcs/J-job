Japan's 顧問 market?* — for free, before committing to anything.

company, search the pool, and record what comes back for: 元リスク管理担当役員, モデルリスク,

expertise, and stated engagement terms. Create `person` cards only where the platform
profile is a legitimate public source.

financial-risk executives — which would redirect the whole stream toward JAFEE, industry
bodies and alumni networks instead. Both outcomes are worth the afternoon.

contract carries a one-year minimum, and the vehicle question is unresolved.
