The cheapest route into the commercial picture that desk research cannot reach.

Register as an expert with a profile built around the three things institutions in Japan,
HK and Singapore are currently short of: model validation and model risk management,
quantitative/HFT market microstructure, and on-chain rate and oracle models. Profiles are
visible only to VisasQ clients and the name is masked at the matching stage, so this is
low-exposure.

**What to watch for, deliberately.** Every booking is a data point on `note-open-questions`
#2 and #4: which institutions are asking, what they are worried about, whether AI model
validation is the live concern the regulatory picture implies. Keep a running note in the
repo after each engagement.

**Settle before registering**, not after:
- Conflict-of-interest and MNPI obligations — VisasQ runs formal compliance policies, and
  being both a paid expert and a prospective vendor to the same institution needs a rule
  decided in advance.
- Whether IPOR Labs and TUJ have any constraint on outside paid expert work
  (overlaps `task-tuj-affiliation-rules`).
- Rate. The platform norm is a few hundred USD per hour; set it at the level the
  positioning implies rather than the level that maximises bookings.
