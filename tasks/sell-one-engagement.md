**The single task that matters more than the rest of the plan.**

Everything currently in this repo is research and structure. One piece of paid work would
settle, with evidence rather than inference: whether anyone pays for this, what they will
pay, how long delivery actually takes, whether a foreign principal can contract for it, and
whether the wedge into further consultancy is real.

Any tier counts. A ¥1.5m fixed-scope review for a startup teaches nearly as much as a ¥10m
bank mandate and can be closed in weeks rather than quarters. `org-garage-bank` is the warmest
route (`task-garage-bank-conversation`).

**Scope it tightly and write it up.** A fixed-scope, fixed-fee, time-boxed engagement with a
defined deliverable protects against the open-ended first project that consumes a quarter,
and it produces the reference case and the reusable protocol that make the second one half
the work.

Do not wait on the vehicle question, the 顧問, or the flagship paper. Those are for scaling;
this is for finding out whether there is anything to scale.
