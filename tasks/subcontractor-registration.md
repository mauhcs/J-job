The route that requires the least selling, and therefore the one to test first.

1. Register on **PwC Talent Exchange** and **Deloitte Open Talent**. Alumni status is not
   required. **Verify first whether the Japanese member firms actually use these platforms** —
   they may be US/UK-centric, in which case the route is a direct approach to a practice lead
   instead.
2. Approach the Japanese ALM/risk boutiques in `org-jp-risk-boutiques` as a subcontractor
   rather than a competitor, once `task-classify-jp-risk-boutiques` says what they do.
3. Position as **the quant specialist who slots into an existing validation workstream** —
   pricing, execution, microstructure, AI/ML model classes. Subcontractors are hired more
   readily when the skill plugs into a multi-person project than when it reads as a solo
   practice competing for the same mandate.

Accept a lower rate than a direct engagement. That is the trade being made deliberately:
margin for not having to sell.

**What this buys beyond revenue:** an observed price and an observed effort for a real
validation — the two numbers that `note-correction-invented-numbers` says are missing, and
which no amount of desk research will produce.
