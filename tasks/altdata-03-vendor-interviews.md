With the published example in hand, the conversation is "here is what I do" rather than "here
is what I could do" — which is the difference between a pitch and a demonstration.

Five questions, detailed in `task-altdata-vendor-interviews`. The one that can kill the idea:
**would a trading desk discount your report because you paid for it?**

If the answer is yes across three vendors, the model has to change rather than the pitch:
pre-registered methodology agreed before results are seen; publication of negative findings as
a standing policy; or being retained by the buy side instead. Better to learn that now than
after building a practice on it.

Start with `org-nowcast` — Japanese, reachable, sells to overseas funds, and therefore has the
exact problem of proving Japanese data to people who cannot assess it.
