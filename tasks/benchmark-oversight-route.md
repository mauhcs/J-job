IOSCO Principle 5 requires benchmark administrators to run an oversight function — a separate
committee or equivalent independent arrangement, composed of a balanced group — that reviews
and challenges the whole determination process. EU BMR recognition additionally requires IOSCO
compliance to be demonstrated by an **independent external auditor**.

Two paid outsider roles written into the rulebook, in the one domain where the IPOR role makes
the profile genuinely scarce.

Establish:

- Do independence rules disqualify someone affiliated with a benchmark administrator? **Check
  this first** — if the answer is yes for rate benchmarks, the route may still be open for
  equity, ESG or digital-asset indices.
- Who administers Japanese benchmarks (TORF, TONA-related), and how are their oversight
  members recruited?
- Does the independent-assessor work require an audit firm, or can a qualified individual do
  it? Read the FTSE Russell terms of reference for how a real committee is composed.
- Ask IPOR's own counsel — IPOR has had to think about IOSCO alignment, so the knowledge is
  already inside the building.

Recurring, scheduled and low-hour, which is the shape this plan most lacks.
