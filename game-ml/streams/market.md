## Question

Which Japanese game companies buy applied ML from outside, for what, and what have they
already built in-house?

## What is established

Adoption is close to universal and very recent — **85.8% of Japanese game developers now use
generative AI**, up from 51% a year earlier (`note-game-adoption`). That cuts both ways: the
category is legitimate and budgeted, and the obvious work is already being done in-house.

The question is therefore not "do they use AI" but **which problems remain unsolved because
they need a skill the studio and its AI vendor do not have.**

## Structure

- **Publishers with research capability** — Square Enix, Bandai Namco, Sega, Capcom, Konami,
  Level-5. Large, slow, partly served by their own teams and by university partnerships.
- **Mobile and live-service operators** — Cygames, DeNA, Mixi, Colopl, GungHo, Akatsuki. The
  richest source of quantitative problems: live economies, gacha, churn, cheating, RMT.
- **Middleware and tooling vendors** — sell to studios, and are a channel rather than a client.

## Current state

First sweep done: adoption data solid, use cases mapped, competitor set identified, buyer list
still generic. The named-company work has not been done.
