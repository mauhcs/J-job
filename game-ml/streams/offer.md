## Question

Which ML services can be sold by a quant with no game-industry track record, and why would a
studio pick him over Preferred Networks, an in-house team, or a cheaper vendor?

## The working answer

Not generative AI. That is where the adoption is, where the bench is deep, and where an
outsider adds nothing.

The opening is the **adversarial and quantitative corner**: cheat, bot and RMT detection;
gacha probability and expected-value verification under 景表法; virtual economy modelling;
survival-analysis-grade churn and LTV; and rating and matchmaking systems. See
`note-game-usecases` for the mapping and `note-game-edge` for why the background transfers.

## The honest caveat carried over from the other projects

The recurring failure has been needing to acquire clients in a market with no track record.
This project must be judged against that from the start, not after another full research
pass — which is what `note-game-risks` is for.
