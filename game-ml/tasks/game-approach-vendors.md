Laboro.AI, Ridge-i, AVILEN and BrainPad sell custom AI and take small to mid-sized projects.
The question is whether game studios bring them adversarial-detection, economy-modelling or
probability-verification briefs — and what happens to those briefs.

Ask: do game companies come to you, and with what? Do you subcontract specialist work? What
does a comparable engagement cost? Also establish what **HEROZ** actually sells to game
studios, since they already combine game and finance AI.

Two outputs: whether subcontracting is a real channel, and the **price floor** — what a studio
expects to pay for a scope like this. The other projects both invented a fee before knowing
that; this one should not.

Sequence after `task-game-cedec-talk` has produced something, so the conversation is a
demonstration rather than a request.
