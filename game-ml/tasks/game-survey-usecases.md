The CESA adoption data covers *generative* AI. Nothing is known about whether cheat detection,
churn and LTV modelling, economy design or gacha verification are outsourced, done in-house, or
not done at all — and `note-game-risks` names this as a kill criterion.

Evidence to gather, cheapest first:

1. **Job postings.** Japanese studios hiring "データサイエンティスト", "不正検知", "機械学習"
   into live-ops or security teams tells you the work exists and is being staffed internally.
   Volume and seniority indicate how well.
2. **CEDEC programmes, last three years.** Sessions on cheat detection, economy balance, churn
   or KPI modelling reveal which studios have these teams and who leads them — named people,
   publicly sourced, and speakers are approachable.
3. **Vendor case studies.** Whether any Japanese AI contractor lists a game client for
   non-generative work.
4. **CESA membership list** — harvest into a pool as the buyer universe.

**If studios staff this internally and never tender it, the project stops here.** That is a
cheap answer to get and it should be got before anything else is built.
