The Japanese game industry's trade association. Publishes the **annual survey** behind
`note-game-adoption` — the 85.8% generative-AI adoption figure and the 32% using AI for
in-house engine development — and runs **CEDEC**, the country's largest game developers'
conference.

Two uses. Its **membership list is the buyer universe** for this project, spanning major
publishers and indies, and should be harvested into a pool the way the micro-insurer
association list was in the other project. And with JOGA it sets the **gacha disclosure
guidelines** that `note-game-regulation` is built on, which makes it the authority on the
compliance question as well as the audience.
