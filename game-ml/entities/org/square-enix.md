Carded not as a prospect but as **the proof point the whole distribution argument rests on**.

Square Enix has announced a target to automate **70% of QA and debugging work with generative
AI by the end of 2027**, pursued through **joint research with the University of Tokyo's
Matsuo-Iwasawa laboratory** — the country's most prominent academic AI group. They also
presented at CEDEC 2026 on generative-AI prototyping for game designers.

**Two conclusions follow.**

A major Japanese publisher will enter a **formal research partnership with a university AI
lab**, publicly. That is documented, repeated behaviour, and it is better evidence for the
academic route than anything found in the other two projects.

And QA automation specifically is **taken** — the largest publisher in Japan is working on it
with the best-known lab in Japan. Do not enter there. `note-game-usecases` points elsewhere
for that reason.
