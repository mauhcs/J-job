Founded 2016, listed on the **TSE Growth market in July 2023**. Sells **カスタムAI** — bespoke
AI designed per client problem — alongside AI consulting, and takes **small and mid-sized
projects** as well as large ones.

**The most plausible subcontracting counterparty in this project.** A custom-AI house builds
whatever the client brief demands, which means it periodically receives briefs outside its own
bench. Adversarial detection, virtual economy modelling and regulated-probability verification
are exactly the sort of brief a generalist custom-AI firm would either decline or want help
with.

Also the best available source for the **price floor**: what they quote for a comparable scope
is what a studio expects to pay.

Same shape as Ridge-i, AVILEN and BrainPad, which should be carded alongside it once
`task-game-approach-vendors` establishes whether the route exists at all.
