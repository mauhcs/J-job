**The closest thing to a proof and a warning at the same time.**

Japanese listed AI company with origins in **shogi AI** — the team behind a program that beat
professional players — which has since built a business spanning **game AI and finance AI**.

That is precisely the combination this project proposes: game domain plus quantitative
finance. It already exists, domestically, as a public company with Japanese client
relationships and a delivery organisation.

**The proof:** the combination is commercially viable and a Japanese market pays for it.

**The warning:** they got there first, they are local, and they are listed. Any positioning
that amounts to "game AI meets finance AI" runs straight into them.

**What to establish:** what HEROZ actually sells to game companies — engine-level game AI,
consulting, or products — and whether the adversarial and economic work in
`note-game-usecases` is inside or outside their scope. If it is inside, the differentiation
argument here needs rebuilding.
