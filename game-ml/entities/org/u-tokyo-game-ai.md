A dedicated game AI research group at the University of Tokyo, alongside the Matsuo-Iwasawa
laboratory that Square Enix partnered with for QA automation.

**Relevant as the academic end of the industry's research channel.** If Japanese game companies
collaborate with university AI groups — and Square Enix demonstrates they do — then the people
in those groups know which studios commission research, how the contracts are structured, and
what they pay. One conversation there is worth a great deal of desk research.

It is also the natural home for a co-authored paper on the quantitative side of game AI, which
would serve as the track record this project lacks.
