Industry body for online games, and with CESA the author of the **self-regulatory gacha
guidelines** requiring disclosure of appearance rates for all items, with compliance routes
including a ¥50,000 expected-spend ceiling or published probability bounds.

Also co-authored **blockchain game guidelines** with MCF and CESA, covering RMT prohibition
carve-outs and flagging exposure to gambling law and crypto-exchange regulation — an adjacent
area where quantitative and regulatory reasoning overlap, and where the IPOR background is
unexpectedly relevant.

**The body to understand before proposing anything on gacha verification**, since its
guidelines define what "correct" means.
