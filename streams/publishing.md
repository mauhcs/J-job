## Question

What do we publish, where, and how does each publication buy a specific piece of
commercial credibility?

## The three legs

**Leg 1 — Venues.** Carded under `entities/venue/`. Ranked by what each one buys:
JAFEE buys domestic academic standing and *membership in the room where the 顧問 already
sits*; the Journal of Risk Model Validation buys the exact keyword a buyer's procurement
will search for; BoJ IMES buys regulator-adjacent legitimacy; CFA Society Japan and
Risk.net events buy practitioner visibility.

**Leg 2 — State of the art.** What is actually unsolved in model validation right now,
so the work is a contribution and not a restatement: validation of ML/LLM-driven models
under the 2025–26 JFSA and MAS AI guidance; backtesting under regime change; validation
of on-chain rate and oracle models, where essentially no methodology exists.

**Leg 3 — The work.** One flagship piece per year plus derivative talks. See
`artifact-flagship-paper`.

## The strategic point

Publishing is not a separate goal from selling. A paper on validating AI-driven credit
models, presented at JAFEE, is simultaneously: the credential, the 顧問 approach pretext,
and the marketing collateral for the exact regulatory pressure the buyers are under.
Every venue choice should be justified by which of those three it does.
