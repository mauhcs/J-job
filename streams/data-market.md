## Question

How big is the market for alternative data, who are the buyers and sellers, how is a dataset
actually bought, and where in that chain does an independent quant researcher get paid?

## The chain, as it actually works

**Data owner** (a company with exhaust data) → **broker / scout** (Neudata, Eagle Alpha,
BattleFin, Amass Insights) → **buy-side evaluator** (a fund's data team) → **purchase**.

The interesting structural fact is that the chain has a bottleneck in the middle: the buyer
must decide whether a dataset predicts anything before paying for it, and doing that properly
is slow and expensive. That bottleneck is `note-altdata-evaluation-gap`, and it is where this
venture's proposition sits.

## Geography

Global first, because the buyers are global and the Japanese market is small but growing very
fast and is under-served. Japan is the differentiated position rather than the main market —
Japanese-language data, Japanese corporate relationships, and a local shortage of people who
can do this work.

## Current state

First sweep done. Market-size estimates are unreliable and the demand-side surveys are not;
the competitive picture is clear; the Japan picture is thin and needs its own pass.
