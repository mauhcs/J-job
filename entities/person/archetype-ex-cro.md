**Profile.** Built or ran the model risk management function at MUFG, SMBC, Mizuho, Nomura
or a major trust bank in the 2021–2026 build-out following the JFSA Principles. Now
retired or in a part-time advisory portfolio.

**What they buy us.** Buyer-side empathy and a live address book. They know which
institutions have thin validation benches, which model classes are quietly unvalidated,
and who the decision-maker is at each — knowledge that no amount of desk research
substitutes for. They also translate our offer into the internal language of the second
line of defence.

**What they cost.** Likely the most expensive of the three archetypes in cash terms, and
the most likely to want equity or a revenue share rather than a retainer. Also the most
likely to have non-compete or confidentiality constraints from their former employer.

**Why this archetype is probably the right first target.** Lower reputational bar than the
ex-regulator, far more commercially useful than the academic — and the regional-bank
beachhead thesis depends entirely on knowing which regional banks are exposed, which is
exactly what this person knows.

**Where they are found.** Conference programmes and panels (Risk.net events, JAFEE
practitioner sessions), published bank risk disclosures naming senior risk officers, Big 4
advisory boards, and outside-director appointments at smaller financial institutions.
