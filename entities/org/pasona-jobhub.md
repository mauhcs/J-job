Formed by merging 株式会社パソナ顧問ネットワーク into 株式会社パソナJOB HUB. **15,000+
registered professionals**, placement possible within about a week.

The distinguishing feature relevant to us: it places not only 顧問 but **社外取締役 and
監査役** — outside directors and statutory auditors. That matters if the vehicle decision
lands on an incorporated boutique, because a Japanese outside director is a materially
stronger credibility signal to a bank's procurement than an advisor, and this is where that
market sits.

Park until the vehicle question is settled.
