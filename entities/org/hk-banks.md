HKMA-authorized institutions operating internal ratings-based approaches fall under
CA-G-4 and CA-G-3, which require validation of rating systems and internal models — and
CA-G-4 explicitly contemplates that independent review can be performed by **external
parties with relevant experience**.

That explicit permission, plus an English-language business environment, plus a shorter
procurement culture than Japan's, makes Hong Kong the plausible **first revenue**
geography even though Japan is the strategic home market.

Mid-tier and foreign-bank subsidiaries are the realistic targets: large enough to be under
internal-models supervision, small enough to lack a deep validation bench. The crypto and
digital-asset venues licensed under the SFC regime are a separate, more speculative
sub-segment where the IPOR Labs background is directly relevant.

## Open questions

- Which HK institutions use IRB and therefore fall under CA-G-4 validation expectations?
- Does HKMA maintain any public list of institutions or of accepted external validators?
