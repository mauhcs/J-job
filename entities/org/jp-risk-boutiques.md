**A correction to `note-competitive-landscape`.**

That note concluded that specialist quantitative risk boutiques exist only in Europe and the
US, and that "none was found operating in Japan." That was wrong, or at least overstated.
Fintech協会's venture member list carries a dedicated **ALM・リスク管理** category:

- **KYCコンサルティング** — https://www.kycc.co.jp/
- **コンプライアンス・データラボ** — https://www.c-datalab.com/
- **ステラリスクコンサルティング** — https://stella-risk.com/
- **rubi labs** — https://rubilabs.io/
- **株式会社レンズ** — https://lensinc.jp/

plus **株式会社EduCare** (https://www.edu-care.co.jp/) in the スコアリング category.

**What still needs establishing** before this is treated as a competitive threat: whether any
of them does *independent model validation* as opposed to ALM advisory, compliance data work
or system implementation. Those are different services and the distinction decides whether
these firms are competitors, partners, or neither.

**The more likely reading is partner.** A one-person boutique cannot deliver a large mandate
alone; a small Japanese risk firm has domestic contracting, language and client relationships
but may lack deep quantitative model expertise. That is a complementary pairing, and it is
also a candidate answer to `task-jp-foreign-validator-legality` — a domestic entity of record
with us as the technical principal.

## Next action

Read each firm's service pages and classify: validation, ALM advisory, compliance tooling, or
implementation. Rank the plausible partners (`task-classify-jp-risk-boutiques`).
