Your own affiliation, and an under-used asset in all three streams.

**As credibility.** "Adjunct Assistant Professor, Temple University Japan" is what makes
the word *external* in "external verification" mean something to a Japanese buyer. It is
also the credential that makes joining JAFEE, proposing a co-authorship, or requesting a
seminar slot a normal peer action rather than a sales approach.

**As a vehicle.** One of the two live options is an academic-backed lab. Needs checking:
what TUJ permits in terms of named centres, outside consulting by adjunct faculty, and use
of the affiliation in commercial material. That constraint set decides whether this option
is real.

**As a network.** TUJ's board, corporate partners and executive-education relationships
reach Japanese corporate senior management — a warm-introduction channel that does not
depend on finding a 顧問 first.
