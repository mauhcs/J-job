Operated by 株式会社顧問名鑑. **The largest pool and the one whose registrant base is
explicitly described as including メガバンク and 証券会社 alumni** — which is exactly our
target profile and the reason this card is P1.

**Scale.** 43,000+ registered executives, 38,393+ contracts concluded, 7,000+ companies
supported, 17 years of operation (executive advisory business started 2009). Positioned
as 総合型 — generalist rather than sector-specialised.

**Commercial structure.** Registration, searching and meeting advisors is **completely
free**; fees start only once a 顧問契約 is formally concluded. Pricing varies with the
advisor's background, know-how and days worked.

**The catch, and it is a real one:** the 業務委託 contract has a **one-year minimum term.**
For a pre-revenue boutique that is a serious commitment to a person you have met a few
times. Use the free search-and-meet phase deliberately — it is the cheapest available
window into who actually exists in this pool — and do not sign until the vehicle question
(`task-jp-foreign-validator-legality`) is settled, since a 業務委託 needs a contracting
entity on our side.
