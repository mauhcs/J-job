source of the most genuinely scarce thing on your CV.

**The differentiator.** On-chain interest rate benchmark and rate-model design is a domain
where validation methodology essentially does not exist yet. That is simultaneously a
publishable research gap (`venue-jrmv` takes any asset class), a credential no Big 4 Japan

disqualifying rather than differentiating. The positioning stream has to decide when crypto
leads and when it is a footnote — the honest answer is probably that it leads in Hong Kong
and with digital-asset firms, and is a footnote in Japanese banking.
