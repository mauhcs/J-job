App-based purchase financing (BNPL): installment payments for online and offline purchases
via bank transfer or auto-debit. Now part of PayPal.

Scale puts it past the startup tier — a serious book underwritten by a scoring model, with
the concentration risk that implies, but without the model-risk regulatory apparatus a bank
of equivalent exposure would carry.

P3 because the acquisition means procurement now resembles a large company's, and the
in-house data science team is presumably substantial. Listed as a marker for the tier
rather than a near-term target.
