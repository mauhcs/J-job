Japan's most visible alternative data company, part of the Finatext group. Launched
**AlternaData**, billed as Japan's first investment analysis platform combining multiple
alternative datasets — supermarket and drugstore **POS data** and **payment data** analysable
on one platform. Reported growing inquiries from domestic and overseas hedge funds, with POS
analysis used to read company performance ahead of reported results.

**Three ways to read this card.**

1. **Comparable** — the shape of a successful Japanese alt data business.
2. **Customer** — a vendor whose datasets need independent evidence of alpha to sell to
   overseas funds, which is precisely the proposition.
3. **Competitor** — they aggregate and analyse, so they may already do in-house what would be
   sold to them.

Their MoF seminar material is worth reading in full: it is a Japanese insider's account of
where this market is going, presented to the Ministry of Finance.
