Japan's largest digital bank by account numbers, listed, underwriting consumer and SME credit
off Rakuten ecosystem data.

Scale means a real internal modelling function and bank-grade procurement — so this is a
year-two target rather than a beachhead. Carded for tier completeness and because ecosystem
data underwriting raises exactly the explainability questions the FSA's AI Discussion Paper
addresses.
