KPMG runs a **named, Japan-domestic model risk management practice** with its own
Japanese-language service page — the clearest evidence in this sweep that MRM is an
established, productised line in Japan and not an emerging one. Globally their emphasis
is credit risk model validation and IFRS 9 impairment design, framed around documented
governance and controls.

**Read for us:** the compliance-and-governance layer is taken. Competing there means
competing on price and headcount against a Big 4 bench, which we lose. The opening is the
work their bench is thinnest on — quantitative teardown of pricing, execution and
microstructure models, and validation of AI/ML models where the methodology itself is
unsettled.

Also a potential *channel* rather than only a competitor: Big 4 firms subcontract
specialist quant review when a mandate needs depth they lack. Worth testing.
