Operated by **ファンズ株式会社**, founded 1 November 2016 (formerly 株式会社クラウドポート),
Ebisu-nishi, Shibuya. Capital ¥210m. Registered as a **Type II Financial Instruments Business
operator — 関東財務局長（金商）第3103号**. The Funds lending-investment marketplace launched
January 2019; Series D of about ¥3.6bn in March 2023, ~¥6.8bn cumulative.

Online marketplace for lending funds, matching retail investors to corporate borrowers.

**Different pressure from the other lenders here.** Funds' capital comes from *retail
investors* under financial instruments regulation, so the credit assessment behind each
listed fund is part of what is being disclosed to the public. An independent opinion on the
screening methodology is a credibility asset facing investors, not only a risk control —
and the regulatory exposure of getting it wrong is of a different kind.
