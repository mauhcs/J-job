procurement is long, a domestic credibility signal is mandatory.

The group's research and consulting arm (Mizuho Research & Technologies, formerly Mizuho
Information & Research Institute) is the more approachable contact and sits in the same
competitive space as NRI and ABeam — potentially a partner or subcontracting relationship
rather than an end client.
