Hong Kong's regime runs through the **Supervisory Policy Manual**. Two modules matter
directly: **CA-G-4 (Validating Risk Rating Systems)** and **CA-G-3 (Use of Internal
Models Approach)**. HKMA's standards for model development, validation and ongoing
monitoring follow the Basel Committee line.

**The commercially decisive clause:** CA-G-4 states that independent review or audit of an
authorized institution's rating systems *can be conducted by independent external parties
which have the relevant experience and knowledge of doing so.* That is an explicit
regulatory blessing of the exact service we would sell — and it is stronger, more
quotable language than anything located so far on the Japanese side.

Worth noting for sequencing: Hong Kong may therefore be the easier first sale, with Japan
the larger but slower prize. The market stream should test that.
