**33,999 registered professionals, 24,772 projects delivered, ¥0 initial fee.** The model
is "pro sharing" — external professionals' experience shared across multiple companies on
a project basis.

Project-unit framing rather than a standing 顧問 relationship. Useful to know as a
comparable for how *we* might one day be engaged — a fractional external specialist sold
by the project is structurally close to the validation service we want to sell — but less
useful for sourcing a door-opener, which is a relationship and not a project.

Also worth noting as a possible **distribution channel** in the other direction: platforms
like this are how Japanese companies buy fractional specialist expertise, and a model
validation specialist is exactly that shape.
