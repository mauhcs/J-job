Dai-ichi Life's digital micro-insurance subsidiary. Regular member of the association.

Interesting middle case: a small, new, digitally-native underwriter inside a major insurance
group. It has the group's scrutiny — and therefore a real internal audience for a model
opinion — without the group's actuarial bench being applied to its novel products day to day.

Also a door into the parent: work done for a group subsidiary is the standard route into a
large Japanese insurance group, and one of the few that does not require a 顧問 first.
