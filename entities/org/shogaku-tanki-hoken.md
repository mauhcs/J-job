Micro-insurers, regulated under the Insurance Business Act since April 2006 (formerly
根拠法のない共済), supervised through a dedicated FSA 監督指針.

**The decisive statistic: 41% have fewer than 10 employees, and two-thirds fewer than 20.**

These are licensed, supervised, actuarially model-dependent financial institutions that are
structurally incapable of staffing an internal validation or model-audit function. There is
no scenario in which a twelve-person insurer builds a second line of defence in-house. The
only available answer is to buy it.

The industry association presented on **internal audit sophistication** to the FSA's own
working group in March 2025 — meaning the gap is acknowledged, at the association level,
in front of the regulator. That is about as clear a demand signal as a segment can emit.

**Why it may be better than the fintech tier:** these firms are regulated, so the pressure
is real rather than commercial; they are numerous; and pricing and insurance-risk models
are squarely inside the quantitative skill set rather than adjacent to it.

## Open questions

- How many 少額短期保険業者 are there, and what does the 監督指針 say specifically about
  model and actuarial validation?
- Do they procure individually, or through the association?
