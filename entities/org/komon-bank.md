Operated by 株式会社顧問バンク. **プラットフォーム型** — you search and contact advisors
yourself rather than briefing an agent. Around 10,000 registered advisors, **55.9% of whom
have held positions at listed companies**.

**Why this is the right first move.** Trial at ¥0, and it takes **no intermediary fee from
the company** — payment goes 100% directly to the advisor. Contracts can be per-project,
long-term, or **成果報酬 (success-fee)**, which is the only structure a pre-revenue boutique
can realistically offer a senior Japanese advisor. It also runs regular matching webinars.

**The strategic use is reconnaissance, not just hiring.** Self-serve search means you can
see *what the advisor pool actually contains* before spending anything — whether ex-risk-
officer and ex-supervisory profiles exist in Japan's 顧問 market at all, and how they
describe themselves. That single question currently blocks the whole advisor stream, and
this answers it for free.

Do this before approaching any agent-type service.
