Japan's leading expert network (listed, TSE 4490; founded 2012 as Walkntalk, rebranded
2015). Connects **500,000+ experts across 190 countries**; ~80% of revenue comes from
arranged 1-on-1 interviews, the rest from surveys, short-term assignments and events.
Offices in **Tokyo, Hong Kong, Singapore**, New York, California, North Carolina and
London — the same geographic footprint as this plan. Expert rates are typically a few
hundred USD per hour. Formal compliance policies govern conflicts of interest.

**This is the card to read twice.** VisasQ is not a place to find a 顧問 — it is a place to
*be* one, and that inverts the problem the advisor stream is stuck on.

Registering yourself as an expert means Japanese and Asian financial institutions pay you
to answer questions about quant modelling, risk and on-chain rates. What that buys:

- **Paid primary market research.** The questions asked reveal what institutions are
  actually worried about, and who is asking — precisely the commercial picture the desk
  research could not reach (`note-open-questions` #2 and #4).
- **Inbound rather than outbound.** The counterparty initiates, which sidesteps the entire
  cold-approach problem and the need for a door-opener to make the first contact.
- **The positioning rehearsed.** Being booked as an independent external expert *is* the
  product, sold in one-hour units.

Consider it market research that pays, with lead generation attached — not a revenue line.

**Manage properly:** expert-network work carries MNPI and conflict-of-interest obligations,
and being simultaneously a paid expert and a vendor to the same institution needs thinking
through before it happens, not after. See `task-visasq-expert-registration`.
