Fintech協会 member in 融資・ローン, lending with an impact-investment orientation.

Impact lending carries a second measurement burden on top of credit: the claimed outcome has
to be quantified for investors, and impact measurement methodology is contested. Two model
surfaces rather than one, and the investor-facing one is where independent verification is
most obviously worth paying for.
