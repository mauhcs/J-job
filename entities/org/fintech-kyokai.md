The industry body for Japanese fintech, with a **published venture-member list** — which is
simultaneously a target list for the startup and SME tiers and a network channel.

**Two uses, both cheap.** As a target list it is public, current and self-selecting: firms
that join an industry association are firms that care about their standing with regulators
and partners, which is the same instinct that buys independent validation. As a channel, its
board and committee members are exactly the profile identified in
`person-archetype-fintech-operator` — operators who have taken a lending or payments
business through Japanese licensing, and who know every other founder in the segment.

FIN/SUM (the Nikkei–FSA fintech conference) publishes a comparable startup listing and is
also a speaking venue worth considering for the publishing stream — a practitioner audience
of precisely the buyers in this tier.
