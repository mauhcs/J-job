Japanese consultancy serving banks and securities firms on regulatory compliance, risk
management and internal control, with a lead position in AML/CFT work for major
institutions.

Their published insight on **model risk governance in the AML/CFT domain** is the useful
signal: in Japan, model risk management has been pulled into practice most concretely
through AML transaction-monitoring systems, not through pricing or market risk models.
Accenture Japan has published on the same theme.

**Implication:** the Japanese market's mental model of "model validation" may default to
AML scenario tuning. Our pitch has to either meet that frame or deliberately reframe — and
knowing which is a question for a real buyer conversation, not for desk research.
