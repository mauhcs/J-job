Insurtech technology company, Fintech協会 member and a member organisation of 日本少額短期保険協会 —
spun out of the justInCase insurtech venture (now ソニー少額短期保険).

Sells insurance platform technology to licensed insurers, which puts it in the same
structural position as the AI credit vendors in `org-jp-ai-credit-vendors`: the models inside
its platform are used by supervised institutions whose third-party model risk obligations
flow back through it.

Also a route into the 少額短期保険 segment generally — a platform vendor knows which of the
123 association members are model-dependent and which are not.
