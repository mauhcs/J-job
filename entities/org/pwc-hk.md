PwC HK published a dedicated MRM compliance paper in July 2024. Two points it makes that
are directly useful to us: **model validation should not be limited to back-testing**, and
authorized institutions are expected to maintain a **model inventory** with scope,
materiality and methodology descriptions per model.

Both are sales hooks. "Not limited to back-testing" is exactly where a generic validation
vendor stops and where genuine quant depth starts. A model inventory is a *list of
addressable work* — every material model on it is a potential engagement, and the
inventory requirement means the buyer already knows how many they have.

Worth reading in full as the best available primer on how the HK market frames this.
