The micro-insurance arm of MICIN, the digital health company. Regular member of the
association.

**The modelling is genuinely hard and genuinely novel:** pricing insurance off medical and
digital-health data, in products that did not exist five years ago, with thin loss history.
There is no established actuarial precedent to fall back on, which is precisely when an
independent methodology review earns its fee rather than ticking a box.

Venture-backed parent, so there are investors who care, and no legacy actuarial department.
