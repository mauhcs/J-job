**The best-defined target set in the whole plan: eight named, licensed institutions.**

All eight were licensed in 2019, launched in 2020, and were renamed by the HKMA from "virtual
banks" to **Licensed Digital Banks** in 2024: **ZA Bank, Mox Bank, WeLab Bank, livi Bank,
Ant Bank (HK), PAO Bank** (formerly Ping An OneConnect), **Fusion Bank**, and **ELE Bank**
(formerly Airstar). Six offer business accounts (ZA, ELE, livi, Ant, PAO, Fusion); WeLab and
Mox are personal-only. Ant Bank lends up to HKD 5 million; ZA Bank runs personal loans,
credit cards and an investment arm.

**Why this converges everything.**

- They are **HKMA-supervised**, and CA-G-4 explicitly contemplates independent review by
  external parties with relevant experience. The permission is already written.
- They are **digital-native**, so credit, fraud and pricing decisions are models, not
  committees — and they were built recently enough to lack a deep validation bench.
- They are **small enough to decide** but regulated enough to have to.
- They operate in **English**, with Hong Kong procurement cycles rather than Japanese ones.

This is the strongest argument in the repo for Hong Kong as the first revenue and Japan as
the strategic home market. Eight named prospects, one regulatory argument, no translation.
