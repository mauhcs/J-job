**The direct competitor, and it should be read before anything else in this space is built.**

Exabel is a platform for exactly the proposed workflow: aligning alternative data signals to
company KPIs, testing predictive power, **signal alpha testing and strategy backtesting**. For
buy-side subscribers it shows **backtest summary results against Visible Alpha ground truth —
at dataset, company or watchlist level — before a dataset is even trialled.** It runs a
**vendor partnership programme** turning datasets into embedded long-term assets for managers,
and markets itself on mapping data to KPIs and building predictive signals **in days**.

That is the proposed business, productised and funded.

**What is left, honestly.** A platform optimises for scale and standardisation; it maps to
equity KPIs against consensus ground truth. What it does not do:

- **Non-equity and non-KPI data** — rates, FX, commodities, credit, crypto — where there is no
  Visible Alpha consensus to test against.
- **Bespoke depth.** Platform backtests are standardised. Regime conditioning, shock and stress
  analysis, transaction costs, capacity, and the *incremental* value against a book already
  running are not platform features.
- **Japan.** Japanese-language datasets, Japanese vendors, Japanese corporate relationships.
- **The vendor who is not ready for a platform** — a corporate with raw exhaust data who does
  not yet know whether there is a product in it at all.

Whether those gaps are a business or a rounding error is the central question of
`note-altdata-offer-design`.
