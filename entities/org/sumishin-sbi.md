Digital bank, and the most concrete named AI-credit target found in Japan: it is **developing
an AI screening model for transaction lending** — credit extended on transaction data — through
デイタ・コンサルティング, its joint venture with Hitachi.

**Why this matters more than the segment cards.** This is a *supervised bank*, under JFSA's
Principles for Model Risk Management, publicly building an AI credit model. Both waves of
regulation in `note-jp-regulatory-driver` apply at once, and the model is new enough that the
validation methodology is genuinely unsettled.

The JV structure is also an opening: a model built in a joint venture with a technology
partner sits awkwardly across two organisations' governance, which is the kind of seam where
independent review is easiest to justify.

Larger and slower than the startup tier — but it is the bank most likely to already know it
has this problem.
