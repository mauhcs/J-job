Tokyo revenue-based financing platform, founded April 2021 by Masaharu Uno (CEO) and Ryo
Ohmori (CTO). **Yoii Fuel** lets companies raise growth capital against *future revenue*,
entirely online, with no equity dilution, personal guarantee or collateral. $5.5M Series A
in December 2023 led by Emellience Partners.

**Why this is a strong target.** RBF has no collateral and no guarantor — the forward
revenue projection *is* the underwriting. The whole business is one model, applied to
early-stage companies with short and noisy histories. There is no local benchmark for what
a good RBF model looks like in Japan, and the failure mode (correlated revenue shortfall
across a young-company book) is a portfolio problem rather than a credit-file problem.

Venture-funded, so there is an investor who cares about the model, and an eventual debt
facility whose provider will care more.
