**The closest structural comparable found so far** — a specialist, non-Big-4 firm selling
fully outsourced independent valuation of structured products and complex derivatives,
staffed with quantitative modellers, financial engineers and supporting BI/engineering.

Why this card matters more than the Big 4 cards: Finalyse demonstrates the *business model*
we would be running — independence sold as a managed service rather than a consulting
project — and proves buyers will contract it from a boutique. Their European footprint
means they are also evidence that this model has not obviously been replicated in Japan.

Comparables to study alongside: Kevin D. Oden & Associates (senior-quant model risk
advisory in the US), HedgeStar (SOC 1-certified independent valuation), LSEG's model
validation and benchmarking service for front-office pricing suites across rates, FX,
commodities and equities.

**Action:** read their service structure and pricing presentation closely. This is the
template to adapt, not invent from scratch.
