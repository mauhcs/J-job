Run by パーソルキャリア株式会社 (Persol Career, TSE Prime), rebranded from i-common.
**39,302 registered professionals, 4,820 client companies, 69 industries and 36 job
categories.** エージェント型 — a consultant interviews you and assigns a match.

Positioned around 実働型 engagement: the professional works alongside the client team
rather than advising from a distance. That is a poor fit for what we want, which is a name
and a network rather than hands.

**Why P3.** Largest and most corporate of the options, which means the most process and
the least suited to a one-person buyer with an unusual, narrow brief. Keep on the list as
a fallback if 顧問バンク reconnaissance shows the profile exists but the smaller platforms
cannot reach it.
