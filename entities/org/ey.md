EY's distinguishing move is **platformisation**: the EY BPM Platform automates model
validation, risk management and outsourcing oversight.

**Strategic signal:** the commodity end of validation — the repeatable, documentable,
checklist-driven portion — is being automated and will keep compressing in price. That is
an argument for positioning deliberately at the end that does not automate: novel model
classes, contested methodology, and the judgement call on whether a model is fit for the
use it is actually put to.
