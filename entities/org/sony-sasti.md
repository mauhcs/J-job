Founded 2016 as justInCase, Japan's most prominent insurtech micro-insurer, since renamed
ソニー少額短期保険 following Sony's involvement. Regular member of 日本少額短期保険協会.

**Why P1 in this segment.** justInCase built its reputation on data-driven and behaviour-based
insurance — meaning pricing and risk models are the product itself. It is also the segment's
most visible name, so a completed validation here is a reference case the rest of the 123
regular members would recognise.

The Sony affiliation cuts both ways: more budget, but also a parent group whose resources are
the FSA's own suggested remedy for small institutions. Test whether the group actually
provides model review or whether the licence stands alone.
