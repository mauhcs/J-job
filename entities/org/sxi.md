Automates and upgrades credit decisioning with AI models across payments and loans. Two
deployments make this card P1: **Mitsui Sumitomo Insurance personal loan products**, and an
**AI credit score project with a major Vietnamese financial services firm**.

The insurance deployment puts their model inside a supervised institution's decision chain.
The Vietnam project shows the segment exports — an independent validation that is credible
across borders is worth more to them than a domestic one, and it extends our addressable
market across East Asia without extending our footprint.

Closest fit in the repo to the "validation as a sales asset" pitch in `note-sme-wedge`.
