The financial-institution arm of Money Forward. Built on account-aggregation technology and
an analytics platform over accumulated transaction data; **developed a proprietary AI credit
model with Orico** to predict changes in corporate funding conditions from cash flow.

**Why P1.** The partner is Orico — a listed consumer credit company under JFSA supervision,
whose third-party model risk obligations reach straight back into this model. This is the
`note-sme-wedge` vendor thesis in its cleanest observed form: validate the vendor's model,
and the validation travels into a regulated institution that was going to have to ask anyway.

Money Forward is itself listed, which means a real compliance function to talk to and a
budget — but also procurement. Approach through the model team, not the front door.
