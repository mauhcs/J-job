Regular member of the association whose entire proposition is built on **健康年齢 ("health
age")** — a computed score derived from health check data, used to price and underwrite.

**The single most model-dependent name found in the segment.** A proprietary score that
determines who gets cover and at what price is exactly the object JFSA's AI and model risk
guidance is aimed at: it must be explainable, it must be shown to discriminate risk as
claimed, and it must not encode something the insurer cannot defend to a supervisor.

A licensed insurer whose product *is* a score, with fewer than twenty staff in all
likelihood. If the SME thesis is right anywhere, it is right here.
