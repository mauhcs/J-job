Fintech協会 venture member in the 融資・ローン category. Mortgage technology — screening
and origination support sitting between borrowers and lending banks.

**The interesting structural feature:** the ultimate lender is a bank, and that bank is
subject to JFSA's Principles and to third-party model risk expectations. Anything iYell's
system decides or pre-screens is inside the bank's model perimeter. Mortgage models are also
long-duration and rate-sensitive, which is squarely in the quantitative skill set.
