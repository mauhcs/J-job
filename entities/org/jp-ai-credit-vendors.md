A live and growing Japanese segment building AI credit models and selling them into
regulated institutions:

- **Money Forward X** — analytics built on account-aggregation cash-flow data; developed a
  proprietary AI credit model with Orico to predict changes in corporate funding conditions.
- **Quants株式会社** — AI qualitative credit scoring for receivables delay and default risk,
  aimed at ventures, new firms and individuals with no published financials.
- **SXI** — credit and screening engines automating lending decisions; deployed for Mitsui
  Sumitomo Insurance personal loan products and an AI credit score project with a major
  Vietnamese financial services firm.

**Why this is the sharpest wedge in the whole plan.** These vendors' customers are banks,
insurers and consumer finance companies operating under JFSA's model risk expectations and
the AI Discussion Paper. Third-party model risk management is specifically one of the areas
the BoJ's August 2026 survey found institutions weakest on. Every one of these vendors will,
sooner or later, be asked by a bank client to show that someone independent has examined
their model.

Selling to the vendor rather than the bank means: a smaller, faster buyer; a founder-level
decision; and **distribution into the regulated institutions as a side effect**, because the
validation report travels with the vendor into every sale they make.

The SXI Vietnam deployment also shows this segment exports — which extends the addressable
market across East Asia without extending our own footprint.
