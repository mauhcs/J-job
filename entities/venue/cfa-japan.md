Practitioner rather than academic audience — buy-side and sell-side professionals, with a
flagship **Japan Investment Conference** plus a regular seminar programme, much of it in
Japanese. Offices in Otemachi Financial City South Tower, Chiyoda-ku.

**What it buys:** visibility with the people who sit one desk away from the risk function,
and a bilingual speaking credit that reads as commercial rather than academic. Lower
prestige than JAFEE or IMES, materially easier to access.

No public speaker-application process was found; the route is to contact the society
directly (info@cfaj.org) with a concrete talk proposal. A talk pitched at "what the FSA AI
discussion paper actually requires of your models" would likely land — it is topical,
practitioner-relevant, and demonstrates the offering without selling.
