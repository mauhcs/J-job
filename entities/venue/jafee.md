**The highest-value single venue in this plan, and it is not primarily about publishing.**

Founded 1993, JAFEE covers financial engineering, investment technology, quantitative and
portfolio analysis, and derivatives. It runs **research conferences twice a year, summer
and winter**; the 65th conference is scheduled for around **August 2026 at Hosei
University's Ichigaya campus**. Participation is inexpensive — ¥3,000 for individual
members, ¥1,000 for students, ¥5,000 for non-members — and it publishes the
ジャフィー・ジャーナル on J-STAGE.

## Why it ranks first

JAFEE is the room where Japanese quantitative finance academia and the megabank/securities
quant community overlap. Its membership and officer lists are, in effect, **the 顧問
candidate pool** — and joining is a ¥-trivial, entirely legitimate way to be in that room
as a peer rather than as a vendor. The Adjunct Assistant Professor title at TUJ is exactly
the credential that makes this natural.

The publishing value is real but secondary to the network value. Both legs are served by
the same action.
