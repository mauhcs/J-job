Japanese demand for external model verification comes in two distinguishable waves, and
they point at different buyers.

**Wave 1 — 2021, the Principles.** JFSA published *Principles for Model Risk Management*
on 12 November 2021, following a June–July 2021 consultation that drew 87 comments from 61
respondents. Scope: credit and market risk models, valuation models, stress testing; the
international reference point is SR 11-7. Effect: the largest institutions built internal
MRM functions. Wave 1 created *internal* capability at the top of the market — which is
why the megabanks are a weak buyer segment.

**Wave 2 — 2025/2026, AI.** The FSA's AI Discussion Paper v1.0 (March 2025) and v1.1
(March 2026), plus the BoJ's Financial System Report annex on generative AI (August 2026).
Effect: a new model class, at institutions that never built a Wave 1 capability, with no
settled validation methodology. Regional banks in particular moved AI into core operations
using customer data over the past year while identifying governance and third-party risk
management as unresolved.

**The conclusion that matters for the plan.** Do not sell into Wave 1 — that demand has
been met, by internal teams and by the Big 4 governance practices. Sell into Wave 2, where
the buyer is under fresh pressure, has no internal answer, and the incumbent solution is
not yet productised. This single distinction should drive buyer targeting.
