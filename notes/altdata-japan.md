**Size, with the same health warning as the global figures.** One forecast puts Japan at
**$525.7m in 2024 growing to $7.9bn by 2033, a 35.2% CAGR**. Treat the level as unreliable and
the direction as plausible. Nikkei reported the market quadrupling in three years as early as
2019.

**The ecosystem is real but thin.** `org-nowcast` (POS and payment data, Finatext group) is the
flagship, reporting growing interest from domestic and overseas hedge funds. `org-jpx-data`
sells through J-Quants and publishes a catalogue including partner companies.
`org-nikkei-altdata` sells data and has covered the category since 2019. The **Bank of Japan
runs its own alternative data analysis programme**, which legitimises the category with the
most conservative institution in the country.

At least one Japanese practitioner has published under the title *"Japan's alternative data
market, left behind by the world"* — worth reading as the insider critique of what is missing.

## Why being behind is the opportunity

- **Data owners are less sophisticated about monetisation.** Japanese corporates sit on POS,
  location, payment, logistics and telematics data, and most have not considered it saleable.
  The value proposition *"there may be a product in your exhaust data"* is novel here in a way
  it is not in New York.
- **Overseas funds want Japanese data and cannot assess it.** Japanese datasets carry
  language, calendar, corporate-structure and market-microstructure quirks that a London data
  team cannot evaluate. A bilingual quant who can is genuinely scarce.
- **The platforms are not localised.** Exabel's ground truth is Visible Alpha consensus; the
  brokers' catalogues are English-language and US-equity-centric.

## The honest counter

Small market, small budgets, slow corporate decision-making, and the same access problem that
dogged the other venture — a Japanese corporate does not hand its data to an unknown foreign
researcher without an introduction. The Japan angle is a **differentiator for selling to global
vendors and funds**, and it is not obviously a standalone market yet.

## Open questions

- Who else sells Japanese alternative data? Harvest the JPxData Portal partner catalogue.
- Do overseas funds buying Japanese data complain about evaluation difficulty? That complaint,
  if it exists, is the entire pitch.
- What does the BoJ's programme actually consume, and does it publish a vendor list?
