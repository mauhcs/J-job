Potentially the best segment in the plan: licensed, supervised, model-dependent, and
structurally unable to staff a validation function — 41% under 10 employees, two-thirds
under 20.

Establish: how many registered 少額短期保険業者 there are and whether the FSA or the
Financial Bureaux publish the register; what the 監督指針 says specifically about actuarial
and model validation, internal audit, and use of external parties; what the 日本少額短期保険協会
does for members and whether it procures shared services; and typical premium volume, to
gauge what a firm of this size can pay.

Read the association's March 2025 presentation to the FSA working group in full — it is the
clearest statement of the gap, made by the segment itself in front of its regulator.
