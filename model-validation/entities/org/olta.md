One of the named players in Tracxn's Japanese alternative lending set. Online
"cloud factoring" — buying SME receivables, underwritten fast and largely automatically.

**The model risk is concentrated and legible:** debtor default, invoice fraud, and
concentration in a purchased receivables book. Decisions are made in hours, which means the
model is the process rather than an input to it.

OLTA has run bank partnership arrangements, which matters for the pitch: a partner bank
carries third-party model risk obligations that flow back to the model behind the product.
