Two public award programmes for financial innovation in Tokyo. Garage Bank took the **JETRO
Award at FINOPITCH 2025** and the **Audience Award at the Tokyo Financial Awards 2024**.

**The use is prosaic and effective: the finalist lists are pre-qualified target lists.** Firms
that enter these programmes are building something model-driven, are willing to be publicly
scrutinised, and are at the stage where an external credential has visible value to them.
Every past finalist is a named, sourced, plausibly warm target in the startup tier.

The Tokyo Financial Award is run under the metropolitan government's programme to promote
Tokyo as a financial centre — the same apparatus as the foreign-financial-firm support desk
noted in `org-finolab`. Worth checking whether that programme offers anything to a foreign
principal establishing a financial services business in Tokyo.
