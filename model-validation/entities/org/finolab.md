Tokyo fintech hub and community, reported at 57 members including 17 international
startups. Relevant as a **network channel** rather than a customer: it is a concentration
of people who have made the foreign-firm-into-Japanese-finance transition, including
ex-megabank figures now in innovation and advisory roles.

Lower priority than JAFEE because the population skews fintech-startup rather than
bank-risk-function, and our 顧問 sits in the latter. Useful mainly for pattern-matching on
how foreign principals have structured Japanese advisory relationships.

Adjacent public channels worth noting: the Tokyo metropolitan team for attracting foreign
financial services firms, and the Osaka Global Finance One-Stop Support Center.
