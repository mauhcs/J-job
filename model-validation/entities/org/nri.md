Domestic think-tank consultancy combining management consulting with system consulting —
strategy through to IT build and operation. Deep incumbency with Japanese financial
institutions and, being Nomura-affiliated, unusually close to the securities side.

A specific MRM/model-validation service line was **not** confirmed in the first sweep.
That matters: if the domestic think-tanks have not productised validation, the market is
served by Big 4 plus internal teams, and a domestic partner slot may be open.

**Two readings, both worth testing:** competitor, or the domestic legal/commercial wrapper
that solves the "can a foreign principal be the independent validator of record" problem.
The second is more interesting than the first.
