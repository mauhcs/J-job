PwC Japan sells **model risk governance framework construction** — note the framing: it
sits in the Assurance/GRC practice, not in a quant practice. The offer is "build the
態勢" (the organisational posture): governance structure, management documentation,
executive involvement, verification procedures before deployment and re-verification
after.

**Read for us:** this is consulting sold to the second line of defence. It is adjacent to,
but not the same as, the deep technical validation of an individual model. A bank that has
bought a PwC framework still needs someone to actually execute hard validations under it —
that is a pull-through opportunity, not a blocked door.

PwC Hong Kong published a dedicated MRM compliance paper in July 2024 (`org-pwc-hk`),
indicating the same practice is active regionally.
