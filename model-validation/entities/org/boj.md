The BoJ matters twice over: as a co-supervisor through on-site examinations, and as a
publishing venue through IMES (carded separately as `venue-boj-imes`).

Its **Financial System Report Annex on generative AI** (August 2026) is the most useful
recent artefact. The survey finding to quote back at buyers: institutions have made
progress on internal rules and information management, but identify **governance,
third-party risk management, and safety/security** as the areas still needing work, and
around half report difficulty with training-data sufficiency and quality control.
Regional banks in particular have moved AI from back-office administration into core
operations using customer data — without, on this evidence, a validation capability that
has kept pace.

That is the demand thesis in the regulator's own words.
