The Bank of Japan's research institute runs a **Finance Workshop** (the 2023 edition, held
online in November, was themed on market frictions and asset price formation) and
publishes the IMES Discussion Paper Series and *Monetary and Economic Studies*.

**What it buys:** regulator-adjacent legitimacy. Presenting here means the supervisor's
own researchers have engaged with the work — which is a different and higher-grade
credential than any commercial venue, and it is the one that most directly answers "why
should we trust this outsider to tell us our model is sound".

No open call for papers was found in the first sweep; access appears to be by invitation
or through established academic channels, which makes this a venue the 顧問 or a JAFEE
connection unlocks rather than one we approach cold. Sequence it after JAFEE.
