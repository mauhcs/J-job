linkable URL** — which matters when a paper's commercial job is to exist before a meeting
rather than to clear peer review.

Standard practice: preprint on arXiv, then submit to JRMV or the JAFEE journal. The
preprint is what goes in the email to the 顧問 and in the follow-up to a prospect.
