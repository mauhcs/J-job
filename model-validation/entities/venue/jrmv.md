The journal whose **name is the service we are selling** — which is the point. When a
prospective buyer or their procurement function searches the principal, a publication in
the *Journal of Risk Model Validation* is self-evidently on-topic in a way that a general
quant finance publication is not.

Scope: implementation and validation of risk models — empirical evaluation of existing
models, pitfalls in model validation, and the development of new methods. It explicitly
takes backtesting studies, stress-testing studies, new validation/backtesting methods,
best practice in model development and deployment, and pitfalls in validation techniques,
across all risk types and any asset class. Credit risk is the main field of application
but it is open on asset class.

Two of our candidate topics fit the stated scope cleanly: validation methodology for
AI/ML-driven models, and validation of on-chain rate models as a novel asset class.
