Risk management for electronic trading, at VP level in Tokyo.

**Possibly the closest role to the actual CV in this entire project.** E-trading risk is about
the risk of the automated execution system itself — kill switches, fat-finger controls, latency,
market access, algorithm behaviour under stress. That is HFT knowledge used as a risk function,
and very few candidates have run the systems being controlled.

VP at a bulge-bracket bank is a credible level, and Morgan Stanley Tokyo operates in English
far more than a domestic group would.
