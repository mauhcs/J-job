**The role that joins the two halves of this repo.**

Second-line-of-defence oversight of the firm's e-trading business: independent identification,
analysis, reporting and escalation of risks from e-trading activity; **analysis and review of
e-trading algorithms, including their controls and testing methods**; setting and monitoring
e-trading risk limits; providing effective independent challenge; enhancing governance and
controls; working across sales and trading, quantitative strategists, business unit risk,
compliance, technology and operations.

**Read that again as a description of the skills.** Reviewing trading algorithms and their
testing methods, as an independent second line, *is* model validation — applied to the exact
systems an HFT background comes from. The `model-validation` project's entire subject matter,
in a role where the candidate has also built the thing being challenged.

Very few people can do both sides. Most e-trading risk officers have never written a trading
algorithm; most algo developers have never sat in second line.

VP at a bulge-bracket bank in Tokyo, which is a credible level and an English-operating
environment.
