**Market surveillance at VP level — and surveillance is the discipline this CV maps onto most
directly outside trading itself.**

Trade surveillance means detecting manipulation, spoofing, layering and abusive patterns in
sequences of orders and executions. It is adversarial anomaly detection against participants
who are actively hiding, with asymmetric error costs. Someone who has built HFT systems knows
what the patterns look like from the inside, which is exactly the knowledge a surveillance
function lacks.

A *technical* surveillance officer role means building and tuning the detection models rather
than reviewing alerts — the quantitative end of the function.

Morgan Stanley Tokyo, VP grade, English-operating. Alongside their E-Trading Risk VP role, the
same bank has two second-line quantitative seats open.
