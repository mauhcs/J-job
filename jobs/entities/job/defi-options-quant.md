Remote DeFi options research role, focused on **increasing the capital efficiency of the
protocol** by adapting capital-efficiency designs and strategies from centralised options
exchanges and other DeFi protocols.

Capital efficiency in a protocol is a margining and collateral problem — how much capital must
be locked against a given risk — which is a risk-modelling question wearing DeFi vocabulary.
Adapting mechanisms from centralised exchanges also rewards knowing how those exchanges
actually work, which is the HFT background.

Options specialisation is the gap: this wants a derivatives pricing and volatility specialist,
which is adjacent to, not the same as, rates and microstructure.
