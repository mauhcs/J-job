**The single most interesting posting in this project.**

AI and data *assurance* — not AI adoption, AI **assurance** — at VP level in a major Singapore
bank. This is the job the `model-validation` project argued would have to exist: MAS requires
independent validation of AI-driven models and nobody had specified how, so banks must staff
the function themselves.

The research done for that project is directly usable here: what the MAS guidelines require,
how JFSA's AI Discussion Papers compare, why validating non-stationary retrained models breaks
the classic playbook.

UOB also advertises **First VP / VP, Responsible AI Specialist** in its Innovation Group. Two
AI-assurance seats at one bank. See `note-job-ai-assurance-wave`.
