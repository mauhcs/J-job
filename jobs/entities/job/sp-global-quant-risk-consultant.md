S&P Global hiring a quantitative risk consultant in Tokyo.

**Strong fit, and an unusual shape.** A vendor-side quantitative risk role means advising
financial institutions on risk methodology rather than running a bank's own second line — the
work is closer to consulting than to in-house validation, which suits someone whose recent
background is research and advisory rather than a bank seat.

S&P also sells risk models and analytics, so this sits at the intersection of model
development, client advisory and methodology — and an international employer is far more
likely to operate in English than a domestic group.
