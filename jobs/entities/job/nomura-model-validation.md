Nomura Group's Risk Management department advertises model validation at **Associate** grade.
Typical requirements across these postings: a master's in a STEM field or equivalent,
professional experience in quantitative analysis with Python, R or VBA, and knowledge of risk
management and financial models.

**Carded as a mismatch, deliberately.** The content is right and the grade is wrong — this is
a step down from Chief Scientist, and applying would anchor the conversation at the wrong
level.

**But the posting is still useful.** It confirms Nomura is actively staffing model validation,
which makes their **Risk Methodology Quantitative Analyst** role — advertised as requiring a
PhD or equivalent in a quantitative discipline — the more appropriate target, and makes Nomura
a firm worth reaching at a senior level through a recruiter rather than through a form.

This is `note-job-seniority-problem` in one card: the advertised market samples the wrong end.
