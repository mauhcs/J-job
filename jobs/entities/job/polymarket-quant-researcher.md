The listing specifies the work: **mark price construction and funding rate design for
perpetual futures.**

That detail changes the assessment. Funding rate design *is* interest rate mechanism design —
the same object as an on-chain rate benchmark, in a perpetual futures wrapper. Mark price
construction is index and oracle methodology. **This is the IPOR skill set, named explicitly,
at the top of the compensation range.**

New York, on-site, is the obstacle. Given "anywhere" is on the table, this is the strongest
argument for a relocation on the list.
