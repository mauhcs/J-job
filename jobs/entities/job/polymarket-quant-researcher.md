**The highest advertised compensation found anywhere in this project: $250k–350k.**

Prediction markets are, mechanically, a market microstructure and pricing problem — order
books, liquidity provision, settlement, and the relationship between probability and price.
That is squarely the background.

New York and on-site is the obstacle. Carded regardless, because it sets the ceiling: the
crypto market pays senior quantitative researchers multiples of what Tokyo advertises, and it
publishes the numbers.

Polymarket also advertises **Quantitative Risk Analyst — Derivatives Clearing**, which is
margin and clearing risk — closer still to rate and collateral modelling.
