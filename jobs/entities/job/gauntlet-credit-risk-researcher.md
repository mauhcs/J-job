**Downgraded from strong to stretch after actually reading the posting — which is the point of
reading postings.**

**The blocker: "Remote first — work from anywhere in the US & CAN."** Gauntlet's remote policy
is contiguous US and Canada only. From Japan this role is closed without relocation. I had
rated Gauntlet a strong fit on the basis of what the firm does, never having checked whether
they could employ someone in Japan.

**The second gap is the discipline.** The requirement is 3–6 years of **credit** risk —
PD/LGD modelling, loss curves, direct lending, warehouse facilities, ABS/CLO structuring,
SPVs, covenants. That is structured credit, not rates or microstructure. On-chain credit
protocols and DeFi lending are listed as *preferred*, not required.

Salary $160,000–195,000 base plus OTE and equity.

**What it does teach:** the DeFi risk firms want conventional credit and structured finance
skills applied to on-chain assets. That is a different profile from rate-benchmark design, and
it reshapes which crypto roles are genuinely reachable.
