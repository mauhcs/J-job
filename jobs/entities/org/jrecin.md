Japan's **official academic recruitment database**, run by the Japan Science and Technology
Agency. Every university faculty opening in the country is posted here, searchable by field
and keyword, with professor, associate professor, lecturer and assistant professor grades often
advertised together and decided by the applicant's qualifications.

**Set a standing search and forget it.** Academic postings carry long lead times and fixed
deadlines; the cost of monitoring is a saved query. Keywords worth running: 金融工学,
データサイエンス, 計量, ファイナンス, リスク.

Already holding an adjunct post at TUJ makes a move to a fuller academic position a known
quantity rather than a career change — and it is the one track compatible with everything else
in this repo.
