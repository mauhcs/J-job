The job boards for crypto and DeFi roles, heavily weighted toward **remote**.

**The best-fitting channel in this project**, for three reasons. It is where the IPOR
background is rarest and most valuable; it advertises senior roles openly rather than through
relationship networks; and remote work removes the Japanese-language and Tokyo-location
constraints that filter most of the domestic market.

**One caution on compensation data.** Aggregate figures for "quantitative risk DeFi" roles are
unreliable — one source puts the US average around $90,579 with a range of $35,000–$119,000,
which is implausibly low for a senior quantitative researcher and almost certainly mixes
junior and non-quant roles into the same bucket. Take compensation from actual postings, not
from aggregators.
