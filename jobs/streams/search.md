## Question

Which roles genuinely fit a profile of HFT and quant trading, risk modelling, on-chain rate
benchmark design, and an academic post — and where are they actually advertised?

## Six tracks worth watching

| track | why it fits | where posted |
|---|---|---|
| `model-risk` | JFSA's Principles drove a hiring wave; the whole `model-validation` project is background research for it | Morgan McKinley, Robert Walters, eFinancialCareers |
| `quant-research` | Direct match on HFT and trading | Selby Jennings, eFinancialCareers, LinkedIn |
| `defi` | The IPOR role is rare and in demand | web3.career, CryptocurrencyJobs, direct |
| `academic` | TUJ post already held | JREC-IN |
| `ml-engineering` | Applied ML, including the game industry | LinkedIn, company sites |
| `advisory` | Expert networks, benchmark oversight, outside-director seats | Covered in `model-validation` |

## The uncomfortable structural point

See `note-job-seniority-problem`. Most advertised quant and model-risk roles in Tokyo are
associate or VP level and would be a **step down** from Chief Scientist. The advertised market
and the appropriate market are not the same market, which shapes where to look rather than
whether to look.

## Current state

Channels mapped and a handful of real roles carded. No applications, no conversations. The
listings here are snapshots and go stale — each carries the date it was seen.
