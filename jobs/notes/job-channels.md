## How to use them, given the seniority problem

**Recruiters over boards.** Morgan McKinley, Robert Walters and Selby Jennings reach the
unadvertised senior market that boards do not. One conversation with a specialist consultant
at each is worth more than months of scanning, and it costs nothing — they are paid by the
employer.

**Boards as intelligence.** A posting tells you an institution is building a function even
when the advertised grade is wrong. Record the *employer*, not just the role.

**Crypto boards as the real search.** This is the one market that advertises senior roles
openly and remotely, and it is where the profile is most distinctive.

**JREC-IN on a standing search.** Academic postings appear with long lead times and fixed
deadlines; a saved keyword search costs nothing to maintain.
