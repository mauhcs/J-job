The `model-validation` project argued that MAS, JFSA and the BoJ had all required independent
validation of AI-driven models without specifying how, and that this would force institutions
to build the capability. That was an inference from regulatory documents. **The job market now
confirms it.**

Currently advertised in Singapore:

| role | bank | grade |
|---|---|---|
| **VP, AI & Data Assurance** | UOB | VP |
| **First VP / VP, Responsible AI Specialist**, Innovation Group | UOB | (F)VP |
| **Responsible AI Specialist** | OCBC | AVP |
| AVP/VP, Cybersecurity — Governance, AI Trust and Assurance | Temasek International | AVP/VP |
| VP, Credit Risk Model Validation | UOB | VP |
| Manager, Valuation Methodology | Standard Chartered | Manager |

Three major banks plus Temasek, hiring into AI governance and assurance simultaneously, in the
jurisdiction whose regulator published AI Risk Management Guidelines with a twelve-month
compliance window. In Japan the same pressure appears in a softer form — AIG advertising an
"AI Utilization Promotion Specialist" in Tokyo, which is the adoption side rather than the
assurance side.

## What this changes

**The thesis was right and the business model was wrong.** There is real, funded demand for
independent validation of AI models in Asian finance — but institutions are **hiring it, not
buying it**. That is exactly why the consulting version could not find a buyer: the budget
exists as headcount.

**Which makes this the most direct route into the work.** Months of regulatory research —
MAS, JFSA's AI Discussion Papers, HKMA CA-G-4, why the classic validation playbook fails on
retrained non-stationary models — is unusually strong preparation for these interviews, and
almost nobody applying will have done it.

**And it is the answer to the seniority problem.** This is a new function. New functions do not
have incumbents with ten years of internal service to promote, which is precisely when an
outside profile with an academic post and genuine quantitative depth can enter above the
advertised grade.

Singapore leads, but the same guidance is landing in Japan and Hong Kong. Watch for the
equivalent roles there (`task-job-watch-ai-assurance`).
