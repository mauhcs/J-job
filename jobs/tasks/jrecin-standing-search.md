JREC-IN carries every Japanese university faculty opening. Academic postings run on long lead
times and fixed deadlines, so the only sensible approach is a saved search that costs nothing
to maintain.

Keywords: 金融工学, ファイナンス, データサイエンス, 計量, リスク. Watch for postings that
advertise professor through assistant professor together and let qualifications decide the
grade — those are the ones where an unusual profile can land above the nominal entry level.

Already holding the TUJ adjunct post makes this a known quantity rather than a career change,
and it is the one track compatible with everything else in this repo.
