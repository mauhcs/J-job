The advertised market samples the wrong end (`note-job-seniority-problem`). Senior roles go
through search, so the fastest way to see the real market is to ask the people running it.
It costs nothing — they are paid by employers.

**Morgan McKinley** — the model validation role that prompted this is now closed
(`note-job-staleness`), which makes the call more useful rather than less: ask what replaced
it, and who else is building the function.
**Robert Walters** — they run a named Risk & Quantitative desk, so they have standing mandate
flow rather than occasional roles.
**Selby Jennings** — for the buy-side quant track.

Ask all three the same four questions:

1. At what level does senior model risk and quant work get filled in Tokyo, and how much of it
   is advertised?
2. **How much operates in English?** This is the filter that decides the whole domestic market.
3. Which institutions are building model risk functions right now?
4. How does a Chief Scientist title with an academic post read to your clients — asset, or
   "not a banker"?

Question 3 also feeds the `model-validation` project, which spent weeks inferring the same
thing from regulatory documents.
