Singapore has three banks plus Temasek hiring into AI governance and assurance at once, driven
by MAS guidance on a twelve-month compliance clock. Japan and Hong Kong are under equivalent
pressure from JFSA's AI Discussion Papers and HKMA's supervisory framework, on a slower
timetable.

Run a standing search for the equivalent titles as they appear: AI assurance, responsible AI,
AI model validation, AI governance, model risk — in Tokyo and Hong Kong, at the megabanks,
trust banks, major insurers and the Tokyo offices of global banks.

**This is the single best-matched work in the whole repo**, because the months of regulatory
research already done are the preparation nobody else applying will have. Being early to the
Japanese and Hong Kong versions of these roles is worth more than anything else on the
watchlist.
