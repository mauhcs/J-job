The best-fitting market is remote DeFi protocol risk, and Gauntlet is its reference firm —
quantitative research on protocol economics, market risk and economic safety. That is what the
IPOR role already is, approached from the risk side.

**Do not apply to a posting.** Read their published research, form an actual view on a piece of
it, and make contact on the substance. In a niche this small, being known to the reference firm
is worth more than a requisition being open this month, and a view on their methodology is the
only credential that matters.

Then set standing searches on web3.career, CryptocurrencyJobs and CryptoJobsList for
protocol-risk and rate-mechanism roles, and card comparable firms as they appear — Gauntlet
should not be the only name in this track.

**Take compensation from actual postings**, never from aggregators: one source puts DeFi
quantitative risk at a $90,579 US average, which is implausible for senior work and clearly
mixes grades.
