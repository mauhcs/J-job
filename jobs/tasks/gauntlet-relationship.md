**Revised after reading Gauntlet's postings.** Their remote policy is **contiguous US and
Canada only**, so both open roles are closed from Japan without relocation. The firm is still
the reference point in the niche, but it is not an application.

What the JDs did reveal is what this market actually wants: Gauntlet's Credit Risk Researcher
requires 3–6 years of **credit** risk — PD/LGD, ABS/CLO, SPVs — with DeFi merely preferred.
Their Quant Developer role wants production quantitative systems, explicitly including
algorithmic trading, at $175k–200k. The second is the matching profile; the first is not.

**Do not apply to a posting.** Read their published research, form an actual view on a piece of
it, and make contact on the substance. In a niche this small, being known to the reference firm
is worth more than a requisition being open this month, and a view on their methodology is the
only credential that matters.

**Find the ones that can actually employ someone in Japan.** Check remote eligibility before
anything else — it is the first filter, not the last. Douro Labs (Pyth oracles) advertises in
Europe rather than US-only, which is the shape to look for.

Other curators and risk firms worth checking for open roles and geography: **Chaos Labs**
(currently nothing listed), **Steakhouse Financial**, **Block Analitica**, **Sentora**,
**Paxos Labs** (advertising a DeFi researcher). These firms set risk parameters for Aave,
Compound and Morpho — the closest commercial neighbours to the IPOR work.

**Take compensation from actual postings**, never from aggregators: one source puts DeFi
quantitative risk at a $90,579 US average, which is implausible for senior work and clearly
mixes grades.
