**Founder and Executive Chairman of Eagle Alpha** (founded 2012; CEO until 2022). Former
investment banker at Morgan Stanley and Credit Suisse, and a long-standing public commentator
on alternative data.

**Why he matters beyond Eagle Alpha.** He drove the **Exabel partnership** (since 2022) that
lets clients move from dataset discovery straight into signal testing — i.e. he has already
thought harder than almost anyone about the evaluation bottleneck this venture targets. A
conversation with him is worth having for the market view alone, whatever comes of it.

Eagle Alpha runs a **London Alternative Data Conference**, which is a more natural first
contact point than a cold message — and a possible speaking slot.

As Executive Chairman rather than CEO, he is likely more available for an interesting
conversation and less able to sign a contract. Treat it as intelligence, not sales.
