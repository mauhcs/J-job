**Founder & CEO, Neudata** (founded 2016, grown to ~60 people). Twenty years in leadership,
sales management, fintech and data innovation for the buy side. MBA, University of Graz; CFA
charterholder.

**Why him.** He is the person who decided Neudata should sell monetisation consulting, and a
CFA charterholder will read a quantitative evaluation report on its merits rather than needing
it translated.

**Why probably not first.** A founder-CEO of a 60-person firm is the wrong entry point for
"could you use a specialist" — that is an operational question. Go to
`person-michael-hejtmanek` first and treat Rado as the escalation if it goes well.

**Where he is reachable.** Active as a conference speaker (SIIA among others), so a conference
is a warmer first meeting than a LinkedIn message. He is also a plausible **podcast or
newsletter connection** — see `note-altdata-discovery`.
