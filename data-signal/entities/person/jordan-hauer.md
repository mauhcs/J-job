Behind Amass Insights, and the author of its blog on **data sourcing and monetization
strategies**. Appeared on The Alternative Data Podcast.

**The most reachable of the named contacts, and the best first conversation.** Amass is smaller
than Neudata or Eagle Alpha, he writes publicly on exactly this subject, and a business contact
address — `jordan@amassinsights.com` — appears in public search results. Verify it from the
site before using it.

**Why he is the right first call rather than a sales target.** Someone who writes a blog about
data monetisation wants interesting material about data monetisation. Approach with the
published worked example and a question, not a pitch — the realistic good outcome is being
written about, introduced onward, or invited onto the podcast, any of which is worth more than
a single engagement.
