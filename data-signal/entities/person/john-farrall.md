Publishes **Alternative Data Weekly** on Substack — running since October 2020, past issue
#300, curating **2,500+ sources** into a searchable archive at TableStakesData.com. X handle
`@alt_data_weekly`.

**This newsletter is, in practice, the search engine for this industry.** Being linked in it
puts a piece in front of the people who care, which is what Google Ads would be trying and
failing to buy (`note-altdata-discovery`).

The route is not a pitch. It is **publishing something worth curating** — a dataset teardown
with real methodology and honest negative findings would be unusual in a feed largely made of
vendor announcements.

Also worth noting **The Data Score** (`thedatascore.substack.com`) as a second newsletter in
the same space.
