Host of **The Alternative Data Podcast**, which interviews data providers, asset managers and
others across the sector — satellite imagery, news crawling, card data and the rest.

**The single most valuable introduction in this project, and it is not a sales contact.** A
podcast host needs a steady supply of guests with something genuinely new to say. An
independent quant publishing rigorous dataset teardowns — *including the negative results* —
with an academic affiliation and a Japan angle is an unusual guest, not another vendor pitching
a product.

One episode reaches the buyers, the sellers and the brokers simultaneously, which is the
inbound problem solved in a single move.

**The sequence matters:** publish the worked example first, then approach with it. Pitching a
podcast on the promise of future work does not land.
