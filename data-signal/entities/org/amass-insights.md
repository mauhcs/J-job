Alternative data facilitator founded 2015, bridging buyers to datasets from thousands of
providers, with a specialism in web data.

Relevant here because their public writing is explicitly about **data sourcing and
monetization strategies** — the same subject as this venture. A firm that publishes on
monetisation is a firm whose clients ask for monetisation help, and that is either a
competitor, a referral source, or somewhere to be a contract analyst.

Smaller than Neudata and Eagle Alpha, which makes them more approachable and more likely to
use outside specialists rather than staff every skill.
