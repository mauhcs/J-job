Freelance marketplace with a **quantitative analyst** category, positioned for interim and
part-time engagements with companies and investment firms.

Low prestige relative to the rest of this plan, and the rate is likely to sit toward the
contractor end of the gap in `note-unit-economics`. Carded anyway for one reason: **it is
inbound**, it costs an afternoon to join, and it is a way to discover what companies actually
ask a freelance quant for — which is market research disguised as a job board.

Treat it as a sensor, not a channel.
