The events layer of the ecosystem. **Discovery Days** deliver pre-scheduled 1:1 meetings
between qualified data buyers and vetted providers, live demos of differentiated datasets, and
a curated base of institutional investors actively sourcing data.

**Relevant to the distribution half of the idea.** The plan included offering data sellers "a
small distribution channel with some folks looking to buy data." BattleFin *is* that channel,
professionalised, with the buyers already in the room. Competing on distribution against it is
not sensible; **being the researcher whose report a vendor brings to a Discovery Day is.**

Also the cheapest way to meet both sides of this market at once — attend before building
anything.
