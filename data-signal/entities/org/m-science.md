**The proof that research-on-data is a business and not a side activity.** Founded 2002, about
**120 employees**, producing data-driven research and analytics across **3,922 public and
private companies** for institutional and corporate clients.

Their model is to sell the *research*, not the data — syndicated analysis derived from
alternative datasets. That is a full-scale version of the proposition, twenty-four years old
and staffed.

**Two readings.** As a comparable it shows the ceiling and that buyers pay for derived
research. As a potential employer or subcontracting client, a 120-person research firm has
analyst work, and its coverage is US-centric — Japanese and non-equity coverage is where an
outsider adds rather than duplicates.
