Transaction and payments-derived alternative data provider, selling real-time consumer and
economic activity signals to investors.

Carded as a representative **vendor-side customer**: a company whose entire product is a
dataset, which must repeatedly prove to funds that the data predicts something. Exactly the
profile the offer is aimed at — and a useful comparison for how a mature vendor presents
evidence, since their buyer's-guide material is essentially their own sales argument.
