Data discovery firm with a catalogue of **1,200+ sources**, forecasting **5,000 sources
available within two years**. Runs conferences, a directory, and advisory for both sides.

Two facts that matter to this venture:

- **They employ data scientists** to clean and structure raw datasets into a state where
  actionable insight can be extracted. So part of the proposed offer is already staffed
  in-house at the largest broker.
- **They partnered with Exabel** specifically to solve the end-to-end problem of *evaluating*
  alternative data. The two biggest names in the chain agreed the evaluation problem was worth
  a joint product — which is strong confirmation the problem is real, and strong warning that
  the obvious solution is taken.

Their supply forecast is the most useful number in the sweep: if sources go from 1,200 to
5,000, the scarce thing stops being data and becomes **a credible reason to believe any
particular dataset matters**.
