Nikkei sells alternative data as a business line, and has covered the market editorially since
at least 2019, when it reported the market quadrupling in three years.

Relevant less as a customer than as evidence of **who in Japan already understands this
category**: a media and information group with a data sales operation, an audience of exactly
the corporates who own sellable data, and the convening power to introduce them. A Nikkei-
adjacent route into Japanese data owners is worth mapping.
