Data scouting and intelligence firm sitting **between buyers and sellers** — introducing data
vendors to relevant buyers and supplying market intelligence to funds about what exists.

**Read their own education material first.** Neudata publishes the most useful public writing
on data monetisation and on how to sell data to investment managers — which is simultaneously
the best available primer on this venture and a statement of what they already do for free.
Anything sold here has to be something their guides tell vendors to go and get.

**Competitor or channel.** They do introductions and intelligence, not deep signal research on
a specific dataset. A vendor that Neudata has introduced to twenty funds and that still has
not closed needs a *reason to buy*, which is a different deliverable. Worth approaching as a
referral partner rather than assuming rivalry.
