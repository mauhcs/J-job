## The bottleneck is real and it is documented

Evaluating a dataset properly means aligning its signals to target KPIs, testing predictive
power, running signal alpha tests, and backtesting a strategy. Exabel — who sell the solution —
describe the problem as **end-to-end**, and market their product on making evaluation
"rapid, low-risk and cost-effective **at scale**", which is an admission that by default it is
slow, risky and expensive.

The strongest evidence is structural rather than rhetorical: **Eagle Alpha and Exabel formed a
partnership specifically to solve the evaluation problem.** The largest data broker and a
specialist analytics platform each concluded this was worth a joint product. The problem is
real.

## The squeeze that creates the demand

- Buyers subscribe to **~20 datasets** on average and can only evaluate a few at a time.
- Supply is heading from **1,200 to a forecast 5,000 sources**.
- Every vendor needs to clear an evaluation gate that is the buyer's scarcest resource.

So the vendor's problem is not distribution — brokers and Discovery Days exist — it is
**producing evidence good enough to be worth a fund's evaluation slot.**

## Why the vendor pays, not the fund

The fund has its own quants and will not outsource an investment decision. The vendor, though,
is trying to make a sale, and evidence of alpha is the sales collateral. **This is a pre-sales
and marketing spend**, which is a materially easier budget to reach than a research budget —
the same structural insight that worked in the other space, applied to a better-fitting market.

## But the obvious solution is taken

`org-exabel` already sells backtest-based proof of value, shows results against Visible Alpha
ground truth before a dataset is trialled, and runs a vendor partnership programme. Eagle Alpha
keeps data scientists on staff to do the cleaning and structuring.

**So the question is not "is there a gap" — there is — but "is there room beside a funded
platform, and where exactly".** That is `note-altdata-offer-design`, and it is the thing to
settle before anything is built.
