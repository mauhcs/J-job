## The confusion, and it is partly this repo's fault

**You were never going to sell data.** You have no data. Two different roles got blurred:

| | the data provider | you |
|---|---|---|
| owns | a dataset | a method |
| sells to | hedge funds, asset managers | the data provider |
| competes with | 1,200 other datasets | evaluation platforms and consultancies |
| gets paid | $25–40k/year per fund client | a fee for one report |

Asking "to whom do I sell the data" and "what advantage do I have over other data providers"
are questions about the left column. You would be in the right one. The competitive set is
`org-exabel`, `org-neudata`'s consulting arm, `org-eagle-alpha`'s data scientists and
`org-m-science` — not Facteus or Nowcast.

The drift happened because the discovery plan — teardowns, a newsletter, a podcast — is what a
data business's marketing looks like. The content is right; the framing made it read as though
the product were data.

## So: is there an edge?

**Three that are real.**

1. **Non-equity.** Exabel's proof of value benchmarks against Visible Alpha consensus, which
   exists for equity KPIs and not for rates, FX, commodities, credit or crypto. That is a
   structural gap, not a gap in their execution.
2. **Incremental value against a live book.** Orthogonality to existing factors, marginal
   contribution, capacity, turnover, transaction costs. A platform answers "does this predict
   something"; a desk asks "does this add to what I already run". Different question, bespoke
   work.
3. **Japan, bilingual.** Japanese datasets carry calendar, corporate-structure and
   microstructure quirks a London data team cannot assess. Genuinely scarce.

**One that is not.** General signal research on equity datasets. Neudata has ~60 people and
analysts organised by sector and geography; M Science has ~120 and has been selling research
derived from data since 2002; Exabel automates the standard workflow. There is no edge there
and no reason for anyone to choose an unknown.

**And the one that decides it: you have no track record in this.** Not one dataset evaluated,
no client, no published teardown, no reference. Every "edge" above is a hypothesis about what
you could do, and a data vendor choosing who to pay is comparing a hypothesis to a company
with a client list.

## The pattern across every idea in this repo

Worth stating plainly, because it is the actual finding of the whole exercise:

- **Model validation** — died on no credential, no demand evidence, and needing to sell.
- **Research as a marketing product** — buyers turned out to be megabanks and major insurers;
  same access problem, cheaper door.
- **Alt data signal research** — incumbents funded and staffed, buyer budgets small, and it
  needs selling.

**Three different markets, same rock.** Every one requires acquiring clients in a market where
you have no track record and no appetite for business development. The ideas are not the
problem; the constraint is. Generating a fourth idea will produce a fourth version of this note.

## What follows

**Bury the alt data *business*.** Not the subject — the idea that this becomes a practice
selling evaluations to data vendors. Against funded incumbents, with a small buyer budget, no
track record, and a sales requirement you have already said you cannot meet, it does not clear.

**Keep the harness and one teardown, as a credential, on a hard time-box.** Weekends, not
months; one published artefact; stop there. It requires nobody's permission, and it is the
portfolio piece for every route where *someone else owns the client relationship* — expert
witness, expert networks, benchmark oversight, subcontracting to Neudata or a Big 4 practice,
teaching. Those routes are in `model-validation`, and they are the only ones in this repo that
survive the constraint.

**Stop looking for the business.** The honest shape here is a portfolio of paid expert roles
attached to an existing career, not a firm. That is a smaller answer and it fits the
constraints, which none of the bigger answers did.

## The question this repo cannot answer

What is actually wanted — income, a business, standing in a field, or a way out of something
else? The right answer differs completely depending on which, and three ventures have now been
designed without it being settled.
