Publishing is not distribution. The same week the worked example goes out, put it in front of
the three people who are the distribution in this industry.

**1. John Farrall — Alternative Data Weekly.** Send the piece with two lines: what it is, and
what is unusual about it (an independent teardown including negative findings). No pitch. He
curates 2,500+ sources; the ask is to be one of them.

**2. Mark Fleming-Williams — The Alternative Data Podcast.** Propose an episode with a
specific angle, not a general availability: *"what an honest dataset evaluation looks like, and
why almost nobody publishes the negatives."* Attach the piece as evidence you can hold the
conversation.

**3. Jordan Hauer — Amass Insights.** He writes publicly on data sourcing and monetisation;
send the piece and ask one question about what he sees vendors struggle with on proof. The good
outcomes are being written about, introduced onward, or put on the podcast.

Then post it on LinkedIn, where this community actually converses, and list on
alternativedata.org and Amass Insights as a service provider.

**Measure one thing:** does anyone come to you within six weeks? If nobody does, publishing
alone does not work in this market and the plan becomes contact-by-contact through
`task-altdata-approach-the-sellers`. That is a real finding either way.
