Discovery Days put vetted data providers and institutional buyers in pre-scheduled one-to-one
meetings. For the price of a ticket and a flight it is possible to meet more vendors and more
buy-side data teams in two days than months of outreach would produce — and to watch how a
dataset is actually pitched and rejected.

Go as a researcher, not a vendor. The goal is `task-altdata-vendor-interviews` plus a read on
whether the evaluation complaint is as loud in person as it is in Exabel's marketing.

Check which editions cover Asia, and whether Japanese vendors attend.
