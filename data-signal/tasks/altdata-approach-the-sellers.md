Runs alongside step 3 of `note-altdata-action-plan`, and uses the same artefact: the published
worked example is the application.

Named people, in the order to approach them:

| who | where | the opening |
|---|---|---|
| **Jordan Hauer**, Amass Insights (`person-jordan-hauer`) | Writes the Amass blog on data monetisation; business address appears publicly — verify on the site | Send the published piece, ask what he sees vendors struggle with on proof. Smallest firm, most reachable, writes on exactly this |
| **Michael Hejtmanek**, VP Corporate Solutions, Neudata (`person-michael-hejtmanek`) | LinkedIn | Corporate Solutions serves data *owners* — the right desk. Ask whether they use external specialists and what briefs they turn down |
| **Emmett Kilduff**, Founder & Exec Chairman, Eagle Alpha (`person-emmett-kilduff`) | LinkedIn; Eagle Alpha's London conference | He built the Exabel partnership to solve evaluation. Go for the market view, not a sale |
| **Rado Lipuš**, Founder & CEO, Neudata (`person-rado-lipus`) | Conference circuit (SIIA and others); LinkedIn | The escalation after Hejtmanek, not the first call. CFA — will read the report on its merits |

Approach as a specialist available for briefs outside their bench — Japanese datasets,
non-equity asset classes, market microstructure, incremental value against an existing book —
never as a vendor pitching a rival service.

**Prefer meeting them at an event.** Kilduff and Lipuš both speak publicly; a conference
conversation converts far better than a cold message, and `task-altdata-attend-discovery-day`
puts you in the room.

Ask directly:

1. Do you use external specialists for evaluation or research work, or is it all in-house?
2. What briefs fall outside your bench today?
3. What does a contract analyst engagement look like — day rate, project fee, duration?
4. Is there demand for Japan or APAC coverage you currently turn down?

**Question 4 decides whether the Japan angle is a specialism or a niche of a niche.** If
several of them say they turn away Japanese work, that is the positioning handed over for free.

Do not approach before step 2 is published. Without it the conversation is a request for work;
with it, it is a demonstration.
