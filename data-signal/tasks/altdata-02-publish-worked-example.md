Run the harness on one public dataset and **publish the result** — a preprint, a long post, or
both — including the negative findings.

**Why this is the single highest-leverage item in the venture.** It is simultaneously the
credential, the marketing, the sales collateral and the proof you can do the work. It requires
no client, no introduction, no entity and no permission. And it inverts the sales problem that
sank the other venture: a vendor who reads it and recognises their own problem contacts you.

**Pick the subject so it does double duty.** A Japanese dataset, or a non-equity one — rates,
FX, crypto — where `org-exabel`'s consensus-ground-truth approach does not reach. That choice
is itself the positioning statement in `note-altdata-offer-design`.

**Lead with the negative.** A published evaluation that concludes "this dataset does not add
anything to a standard book, and here is exactly why" establishes independence more powerfully
than any promotional result, and independence is the entire reason a desk would read a
vendor-commissioned report at all.

The academic affiliation goes on the byline. It is worth more here than anywhere else in this
repo.
