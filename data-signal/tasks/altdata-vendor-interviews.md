**Do this before designing anything.** The previous venture built a tier model, a pricing
table and a strategy on an unverified demand premise. The equivalent premise here is that data
vendors pay outsiders to prove their datasets work, and it is equally unverified.

Five questions, to three or more vendors:

1. What do you currently do to show a fund your data predicts something?
2. Who does that work — in-house, a platform, a consultant — and what does it cost you?
3. What happens when a fund says no? Do you learn why?
4. Would an independent report change the conversation, or would a desk dismiss it because you
   paid for it?
5. What is your pre-sales budget, and who signs it off?

**Question 4 is the one that can kill the idea**, and it should be asked plainly. A report
commissioned by the vendor has an obvious conflict; the answer determines whether the
deliverable must be structured differently — for instance commissioned by the vendor but
methodologically pre-registered, or paid on a basis that survives the conflict question.

Targets: `org-nowcast` in Japan, plus vendors met through `org-battlefin` Discovery Days or
introduced by `org-neudata` and `org-eagle-alpha`. Attending one Discovery Day is likely the
cheapest way to have all three conversations in a day.
