Deliberately last, and deliberately unpriced until the first three are done — the previous
venture invented a fee table before knowing either the effort or the demand, and it did not
survive contact.

Once the harness exists and the effort is measured, decide between:

- **Fixed-scope report per dataset.** Simple, repeatable, and the harness makes it cheaper each
  time.
- **A tiered screen.** A cheap first-pass integrity-and-viability screen, with a full evaluation
  only if it survives — which matches how vendors actually triage and lowers the entry price.
- **Retainer for a vendor with many datasets.** Recurring, which is the shape this profile most
  needs.
- **Scorecard or subscription**, rated consistently across datasets. Compounds hardest,
  competes most directly with `org-exabel`.

Price it from observed effort × the day rate in `note-unit-economics`, and not before.
